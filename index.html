Badi

Badi is a private personal assistant. One person runs it for their own use, on their own server. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

It connects to its owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar. It reads them to sort email, answer its owner's questions about their mail and calendar, and send them a morning brief. It can also write emails its owner asks for, and each one is only sent after they approve its exact text.
