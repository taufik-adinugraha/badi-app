Badi privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Badi is a private assistant used only by the person who runs it. The only Google account connected to it is theirs, so this policy describes what happens to their own data.

What it can access

It can read Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, and the owner's YouTube channel's numbers and comments. It can also create drafts and send email, but it only sends an email after the owner has approved its exact recipients and text. It can add labels to email and move email to Trash, which it only does to tidy the owner's inbox, and it reports what it moved. It can merge duplicate contacts, add calendar events, and edit or create Google Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms, and only does so after the owner approves each change. It doesn't change or remove calendar events that are already there, or invite anyone to one. It can't change the channel, delete a file, change who a file is shared with, or delete email for good.

How the data is used

To sort email, answer the owner's questions about their mail, calendar, files, contacts and channel, report on that channel, write them a morning brief, summarize their spending from the transaction emails and statements their banks send, find subscriptions they may have forgotten, write emails the owner asks for, and tidy their inbox. It isn't used for advertising, and it isn't sold.

Who else handles it

The text of emails Badi reads is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be processed. Email that Gmail files under Promotions or Social isn't sent, and neither are security emails. Bank emails and statements are read by plain code, with only shop names sent to be categorized. If the owner asks about their spending in chat, the figures for that answer are sent too. To write an email, what the owner asked for and any email being answered are sent to the same API. So is the text of a Drive file when the owner asks about it, a contact's details when the owner asks Badi to look someone up, and YouTube comments, to summarize them. Replies, briefs and drafts reach the owner through Telegram. The data isn't shared with anyone else.

What's kept

The sign-in token is kept on the owner's private server. So are the sender, subject and a one-line summary of each email, for 30 days, and for up to two months longer in encrypted backups. The date, amount, kind and shop or person from bank emails and statements are kept for two years. Briefs and conversations with Badi stay in its conversation log. The text of an email it writes is kept for 30 days, and for up to two months longer in encrypted backups. A copy of contacts it merges is kept for 30 days, so the merge can be undone, and so is the text of a change it proposes to a file. The sender, subject and reason for each email it moves are kept for 30 days, and for up to two months longer in encrypted backups. The text of an email or file it reads isn't kept.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
