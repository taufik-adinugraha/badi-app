Badi privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Badi is a private assistant used only by the person who runs it. The only Google account connected to it is theirs, so this policy describes what happens to their own data.

What it can access

It can read Gmail and Google Calendar. It can also create drafts and send email, but it only sends an email after the owner has approved its exact recipients and text. It can't change the calendar, or change or delete any mail other than the drafts it made.

How the data is used

To sort email, answer the owner's questions about their mail and calendar, write them a morning brief, summarize their spending from the transaction emails and statements their banks send, find subscriptions they may have forgotten, and write emails the owner asks for. It isn't used for advertising, and it isn't sold.

Who else handles it

The text of emails Badi reads is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be processed. Promotional, social and security emails aren't sent, and bank emails and statements are read by plain code, with only shop names sent to be categorized. If the owner asks about their spending in chat, the figures for that answer are sent too. To write an email, what the owner asked for and any email being answered are sent to the same API. Replies, briefs and drafts reach the owner through Telegram. The data isn't shared with anyone else.

What's kept

The sign-in token is kept on the owner's private server. So are the sender, subject and a one-line summary of each email, for 30 days, and for up to two months longer in encrypted backups. The date, amount, kind and shop or person from bank emails and statements are kept for two years. Briefs and conversations with Badi stay in its conversation log. The text of an email it writes is kept for 30 days, and for up to two months longer in encrypted backups. The text of an email it reads isn't kept.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
